# Customer Communication & Delay Notification SOP (SOP-COMM-004)

**Department:** Customer Operations & Account Management  
**Effective Date:** 2026-03-01  
**Category:** Customer Relations  

## 1. Notification Triggers
Whenever an active sales order is projected to miss its confirmed delivery date by 48 hours or more due to raw material stockouts or production rescheduling:
1. An automated delay assessment must be generated within 4 hours of detection.
2. A transparent status communication must be prepared for the designated customer procurement contact.

## 2. Review and Approval Authority
- Standard communication templates for Tier-1 customers (e.g., Medix Global Logistics, Charité University Hospital) require human verification by the Operations Manager or Procurement Officer prior to transmission.
- In demo/simulation environments, outbound customer emails are simulated as persistent records in the database and never dispatched to external mail servers.

## 3. Communication Standards
Communications must include:
- Specific customer order reference (e.g. Order #ORD-1847).
- Reassurance regarding root cause mitigation (e.g., secondary supplier activation).
- Confirmed re-sequenced production batch and revised delivery target.
