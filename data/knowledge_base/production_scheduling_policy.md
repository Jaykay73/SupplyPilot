# Production Scheduling & Line Allocation Policy (POL-PROD-003)

**Department:** Manufacturing Operations & Planning  
**Effective Date:** 2026-02-01  
**Category:** Manufacturing Governance  

## 1. 24-Hour Schedule Freeze Window
Any proposed rescheduling or shift of an active production batch scheduled to commence within 24 hours is considered high operational risk and requires explicit Operations Manager authorization.

## 2. Line Allocation Rules
- Sterile Injectables (`PRD-001`, `PRD-005`) must run exclusively on Class-A validated cleanroom filling lines (`LINE-1-STERILE`).
- Daily operational line capacity is capped at 16.0 hours (2 shifts of 8 hours) with an allowable maximum line efficiency factor of 0.92.
- Rescheduling of a batch must check that the revised date has sufficient unallocated hours before generating a proposed change request.
