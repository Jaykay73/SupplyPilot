# Purchase Approval Policy (POL-PROC-001)

**Department:** Global Procurement & Supply Chain  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Category:** Financial Governance & Procurement Authorization  

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy defines the authoritative spending thresholds and approval authorization hierarchy for raw materials, active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), packaging, and operational consumables within PharmaPulse Synthetics.

## 2. Authoritative Approval Thresholds
All proposed purchase requisitions must adhere strictly to the following approval tier structure:

- **Tier 1 (Automated / Expedited Execution): < €5,000 EUR**
  - Eligible for direct autonomous execution by the Operations Agent or single-click confirmation by a Procurement Officer.
  - Must meet standard qualification and dual-sourcing requirements.

- **Tier 2 (Procurement Officer Approval): €5,000 EUR to €25,000 EUR**
  - Requires explicit human review and approval by a designated Procurement Officer.
  - The agent must prepare an action proposal, document supplier lead times, and present verified stock shortages.

- **Tier 3 (Operations Manager Approval): > €25,000 EUR**
  - Requires executive sign-off by the Operations Manager or Chief Operating Officer.
  - Mandatory for emergency raw material orders or non-standard contract amendments exceeding €25,000 EUR.

## 3. Override Invariants
Probabilistic risk assessment tools (such as Jev) or AI confidence scores may escalate a low-value requisition to human review if anomalous patterns are detected. However, **no AI system or external model may downgrade an approval threshold mandated by this policy**.
